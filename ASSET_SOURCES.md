# Asset sources

## Rosemary botanical

The decorative rosemary foliage is adapted from Otto Wilhelm Thomé's 1885 illustration of *Rosmarinus officinalis* in *Flora von Deutschland, Österreich und der Schweiz*.

- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Illustration_Rosmarinus_officinalis0.jpg
- License status: Public domain, Public Domain Mark 1.0
- Site adaptation: The rosemary branches were isolated, recolored, and cropped for use as low-opacity decorative foliage.
