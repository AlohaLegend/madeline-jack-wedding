# Asset sources

## Rosemary botanical

The decorative rosemary foliage is adapted from Otto Wilhelm Thomé's 1885 illustration of *Rosmarinus officinalis* in *Flora von Deutschland, Österreich und der Schweiz*.

- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Illustration_Rosmarinus_officinalis0.jpg
- License status: Public domain, Public Domain Mark 1.0
- Site adaptation: The rosemary branches were isolated, recolored, and cropped for use as low-opacity decorative foliage.

## Dawnridge sun impression

The decorative sun is an original generated adaptation of the starburst ornament on the Dawnridge facade, styled to share the rosemary illustration's faint, pressed-paper character.

- Visual references: `public/images/dawnridge-entry-hq.webp` and `public/images/rosemary-thome.png`
- Generated with: OpenAI image generation
- Site adaptation: Transparent background, resized to 900 by 900 pixels, and used only as a low-opacity background impression.
