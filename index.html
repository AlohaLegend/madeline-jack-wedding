How we got here

These two lovebirds have been in each other’s lives for a full decade now. They met on the first day of college in their dear friend Evan’s dorm room and quickly hit it off (as friends). Both are romantics who love a slow burn, so for three years, they were friends, classmates, and collaborators. Madeline was always gracious enough to help Jack with his homework, especially when he hadn’t done any of it the night before.

The stars finally aligned for Jack one cold February night when his best friends, Jake and Demitri, came to visit the city. At the recently closed Kind Regards (RIP), under the shine of a disco ball, the two finally got together, and the rest is history.

From road trips across the country and traveling around the world to surviving a pandemic at Peter and Tracy’s house, recording songs in Jack’s childhood bedroom, and even raising their beloved puppy together, Jack and Madeline have spent the last seven years growing together and supporting one another as they pursue their dreams. <3

The place we chose.

Jack and I cannot believe we get to share the evening with you at our dream venue! Dawnridge is the former home of set & costume designer Tony Duquette. Built in 1949, the estate carries the spirit of his Hollywood era through ornate, hand-painted rooms, tangled gardens, and art Duquette collected and created over his lifetime. Around Beverly Hills

Seven places worth leaving the hotel for, from morning coffee to a quiet hour in the gardens.

01
Coffee & breakfast

Chaumont

French pastries, coffee, and a straightforward breakfast stop on South Beverly Drive.

↗︎02
Lunch & provisions

The Cheese Store of Beverly Hills

A neighborhood institution since 1967, now with a cafe for excellent sandwiches and a deep counter of cheese, wine, and picnic supplies.

↗︎03
Dinner

La Dolce Vita

A small, old-school Italian dining room in Beverly Hills with red booths, low light, and a menu built for a long dinner. Reserve ahead.

↗︎04
Gardens

Greystone Mansion

Free public grounds at a historic Beverly Hills estate. Check the city site for hours and private-event closures.

↗︎05
Garden tour

Virginia Robinson Gardens

A historic estate and botanical garden. Tours require advance reservations and walk-ins are not available.

↗︎06
Art & architecture

Schindler House

A landmark 1922 house and exhibition space in West Hollywood. Timed tickets are recommended, and it is a rewarding stop for design lovers.

↗︎07
Sunday morning

Beverly Hills Farmers’ Market

A certified neighborhood market held every Sunday from 8 AM to 1 PM, with produce, bread, prepared food, and free two-hour parking.

A car is useful if you plan to explore Los Angeles, but it is not necessary for the wedding itself. Rideshare is the simplest option for getting to and from Dawnridge. Please do not plan to drive after the reception.

Rideshare is encouraged, as parking is limited. Details will be posted closer to the date.

07What should I know about accessibility?

The venue features multiple levels and stairs, and the evening will take place both indoors and outdoors. We recommend wearing low heels or comfortable shoes to navigate the space. If you have any accessibility concerns or questions about the venue layout, please let us know in advance so we can help make your evening as comfortable as possible.

08Which airport should I use?

LAX has the widest range of flights. Hollywood Burbank Airport is smaller and can be an easier option when a nonstop flight is available. Travel time from either airport depends heavily on Los Angeles traffic.

09Where should I stay?

We recommend staying in or just outside Beverly Hills. The Peninsula is the classic luxury option, The Maybourne is the most walkable, Hotel 850 SVB is the small design-led pick, and Avalon is the most relaxed. More details are in the travel section above.
